# zkFMI brand

Concept E1: decide, instruct, settle. Three layers, one value carried through
them sealed. The top layer is the market (bid and ask depth, amber and
periwinkle), the middle layer is the payment instruction (the sealed teal
hexagon), the bottom layer is the ledger where cash (coin) and securities
(certificate) move together: delivery versus payment. The dashed teal spine is
the same commitment passing through every layer, never opened.
